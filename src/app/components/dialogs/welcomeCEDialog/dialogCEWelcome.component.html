The MAPS tool cost and effectiveness module allows users to estimate the cost, effectiveness, and cost-effectiveness of large-scale food fortification (LSFF), biofortification (via crop breeding), and agronomic biofortification intervention.

Detailed cost models are provided for Ethiopia and Malawi; however, these models can be used as the basis for modeling interventions in other geogrpahies.

Please contact us using the ‘Submit Feedback’ button on the right side of the screen, or by email to feedback@micronutrient.support for further information. We will update this message as our functionality is finalised for use, and new features are added.

If you would like to request, or up-vote, features operationalised in the tool, please use the Feature Request Portal to do this.
